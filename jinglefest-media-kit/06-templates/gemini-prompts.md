# Making new art with Gemini

Use **gemini-3.1-flash-image**, or Gemini in the app with the image tool. Attach one existing scene from `04-backgrounds` as the style reference.

Never ask Gemini to write words. Image models get dates and phone numbers wrong. Make the picture with no text, then add the words yourself.

## Step 1: paste this style block first

> Bold, joyful sticker illustration for a Christmas carnival in South India. Thick dark outlines with a cream sticker edge around characters, flat saturated colours, hard offset shadows, a retro carnival flyer feeling. Palette: tomato red #e5142b, marigold #ffbf1f, hot pink #ff2e7e, pine green #0b5a43, mint #38e0ac, cream #fff3d6, near-black ink #1b0f12. Radiating two-tone sunburst background. Any people are original cartoon characters, friendly, Indian, with warm brown skin.
ABSOLUTELY NO text, letters, numbers, words, logos, signs or watermarks anywhere in the image, including on clothing, posters and banners. Fill the whole frame edge to edge, with no border.

## Step 2: add your scene

Describe the scene and end with this line so there's room for a headline:

> Keep the top 58% of the image as plain sunburst with nothing in it, reserved for a headline. All characters and objects sit in the bottom 42%.

## Step 3: check it

- Zoom in and look for stray letters, especially on signs, awnings, clothes and clock faces.
- Gemini sometimes adds a border or a coloured band. Crop it off or try again.
